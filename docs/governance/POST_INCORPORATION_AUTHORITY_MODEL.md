# Post-Incorporation Authority Model

GP: GP-FW-04B - Incorporacao Institucional do Nucleo Constitucional
Date: 2026-07-20

## Authority After Incorporation

1. ICFACTORY Constitution and Constitutional Lexicon at repository root.
2. Governance architecture and project constitution template under `governance/`.
3. Methodological Custody documents under `docs/governance/`.
4. Scientific maturity and knowledge evolution documents under `docs/governance/`.
5. Gates and audit reports under `docs/governance/` and `docs/audits/`.
6. Research hypotheses and Discovery lifecycle under `research/`.
7. Historical origin records in HANDA_CORE remain evidence, not operational dependency.

## Precedence

Constitutional authority prevails over scientific governance. Scientific gates do not alter constitutional status unless a V to C process performs effective incorporation into the constitutional authority.

Historical reports remain valid as records of the state at the time they were authored. Future state documents should refer to this repository as the official framework authority and to HANDA_CORE as historical origin.

## Working Tree Rule

Working tree content is evidence of local existence but not reproducible institutional authority until committed. GP-FW-04B requires the incorporated files and required custodial documents to be committed together after verification.

## Baseline Boundary

The model created by GP-FW-04B enables a future reexecution of GP-FW-04, but does not itself approve Baseline v1.0.
